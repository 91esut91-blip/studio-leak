About Us

Who we are

StudioLeak uncovers the true stories behind the music you grew up with — recording studio secrets, on-stage incidents, songs written for someone else, and facts that rarely make the headlines.

Every video is a short-form commentary piece built on publicly documented interviews, verified music journalism, and archival news sources (Songfacts, official artist interviews, press coverage). We do not use full songs or official music videos — clips are limited to interview footage, documentary excerpts, and archival material used for commentary and educational purposes.

Our Sources

  • Publicly documented interviews
  • Verified music journalism
  • Archival news & press coverage
  • Songfacts & official artist interviews
